Bird Flow Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Developer: Digital Studio Records

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Bird Flow (the "App"), published by Digital Studio Records ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information. In short: Bird Flow does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and does not access your camera or microphone.

The App does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no third-party tracking tools, and it requests no special Android permissions.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the App saves the following game data only on your own device:

  • Game progress such as coins, best scores, unlocked birds, worlds and trails, and equipped items
  • Game statistics, achievements, daily rewards and mission progress
  • Settings such as sound, music and vibration preferences

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, access it or recover it.

3. Sharing your score

The App includes an optional "Share" button. If you tap it, the App opens your device's standard share menu with a short message about your score. You choose which app (for example, a messaging or social media app) receives that message. We do not receive any information when you share, and what happens next is governed by the privacy policy of the app you choose.

4. How we use information

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use, sell, rent or share it for any purpose, including advertising.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete all of it at any time by either:

  • Uninstalling Bird Flow, or
  • Opening your device's Settings → Apps → Bird Flow → Storage → Clear data

Deleting this data permanently resets your progress, and it cannot be restored. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

6. Security

Your game data is stored using your device's standard app storage, which other apps cannot access under Android's normal security protections. No data is transmitted over a network.

7. Children's privacy

Bird Flow is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will address it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, or to object to its use. Because Bird Flow collects no personal information, we hold no such data. You can delete all game data stored on your device at any time as described in Section 5.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's features change. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If a future version of the App begins collecting any information, this policy will be updated before that change takes effect.
